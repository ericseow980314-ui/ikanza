Our story

One fisherman. More tables. The flavours of Semporna.

Ikanza began with a simple wish: help more Malaysian households discover the fish and flavours he knows best.

Good fish deserve a clearer way to reach your table.
Where it began

Closer to the source. Clearer at the table.

For a fisherman in Semporna, seafood is part of home. He wanted families beyond Sabah to enjoy its flavours, too.

The Semporna farmer previously relied on one buyer who supplies businesses. Ikanza is being built to bring his fish closer to households in Peninsular Malaysia, with clearer product, price and delivery details before anyone orders.

We want it to be easier to understand the fish, see the full cost and know what to expect before ordering. Our first focus is homes in Peninsular Malaysia.
