Our story

From Semporna’s small fishers to more Malaysian tables.

Ikanza is exploring a clearer way for Peninsular households to discover wild-caught fish from Semporna.

Good fish deserve a clearer way to reach your table.
Where it began

Closer to the source. Clearer at the table.

Our Semporna partner buys wild-caught fish from small local fishers and sells it beyond the area. That local network is the starting point for Ikanza.

Our partner has shared the fish range shown in the shop. Before any order opens, each sale listing still needs the exact species name where unclear, current availability, weight, condition, price and delivery plan.

Our first focus is fish for households in Peninsular Malaysia. The priced fish and other seafood cards are market-based examples for trying the shop; they are not confirmed supplier stock.
