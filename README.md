# Ikanza storefront preview

This public static site demonstrates a proposed fish-first shopping experience for Peninsular Malaysia. It is shaped by a Semporna partner who buys wild-caught fish from small local fishers. The partner has supplied a fish-name list, shown separately from the 25 market-priced example products. The site also includes a browser-only cart and checkout, sample account and tracking flows, a wholesale enquiry preview, and three partner-shared catch videos.

**No orders, payments, customer accounts, courier updates or wholesale enquiries are sent.** The partner fish names do not establish current stock or sale-ready product details. Some trade names still need exact species checks, and weights, condition, prices, offers and delivery need confirmation. The 25 priced cards, their images and the sample checkout are illustrative; each priced card links to a Malaysian market listing used as a guide reference. The videos are partner-shared footage, not a live view of current stock.

The site is available in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. See [Privacy & demo data](privacy/) for the browser storage used by the preview and how to clear it.
