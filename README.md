# Ikanza storefront preview

This public static site demonstrates a proposed fish-first shopping experience for Peninsular Malaysia. It is shaped by a Semporna partner who buys wild-caught fish from small local fishers. It includes example products, pack sizes, guide prices, a browser-only cart and checkout, sample account and tracking flows, and a wholesale enquiry preview.

**No orders, payments, customer accounts, courier updates or wholesale enquiries are sent.** The catalogue, prices, offers, imagery and delivery fees are illustrative. Each product page links to a Malaysian market listing used as a price reference; none is a confirmed Ikanza supplier offer.

The site is available in English, Bahasa Melayu and Chinese. See [Privacy & demo data](privacy/) for the browser storage used by the preview and how to clear it.
